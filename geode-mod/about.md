# RGB Sync

Tells the **RGB Sync** desktop app what is happening in your level so your keyboard and
room lights can follow the game: progress, game mode, gravity, speed, the background and
player colors, the music pulse, deaths, restarts, checkpoints and completions.

- Windows only, Geometry Dash 2.2091, Geode 6.
- Sends small messages to `127.0.0.1:47831` (UDP, loopback only). Nothing leaves your computer.
- Has no menus or settings and never changes gameplay.

You need the RGB Sync desktop app running for anything to happen. Without it the mod does nothing.
